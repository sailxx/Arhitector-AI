08 / FAQ

Frequently
asked

Why are prices so low?+

We launched recently and are building our portfolio. Early clients get launch pricing in exchange for a review. The quality is the same as at full price.

How do I pay? Do I get a receipt?+

I'm a registered self-employed professional in Russia: you get an official receipt for every payment. 50% upfront, the rest on delivery. The payment method for international clients is agreed individually. Full terms: Terms of Service.

Do you work with clients outside Russia?+

Yes. We communicate in English or Russian, and international prices are in USD.

What are the timelines?+

Creatives from 24 hours, ad videos from 2 days, websites and AI bots from 5 days. We fix exact dates before starting.

Doesn't AI video look cheap?+

Not when it's directed. We write a script, match the brand style, generate, pick the best takes and polish them in editing.

Who pays for AI services and hosting?+

Generation for videos and creatives is included. For bots and automations, API and server costs are billed directly to your account — usually small amounts; we'll estimate them upfront.
